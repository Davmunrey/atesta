The 26 common guides consist of 20 documentary reviews and six technical configuration
examples. They are shared, not multiplied by framework. A preparation reference is not a
normative mapping. ENS, NIS2, GDPR (Art. 32 to 34) and DORA contain requirement-to-control candidates, all
unreviewed, and a coverage ledger of their source (`coverage.yaml`). Framework-specific scope, applicability, limitations and sources are in pack.yaml.

## How to use a template

1. Establish jurisdiction, entity/system scope and the applicable version with the responsible
   reviewer. Do not infer applicability from company size, cloud provider or a sales request alone.
2. Inventory existing evidence and settings against suggested preparation topics. Record
   missing evidence, failed observations and unassessed areas separately; none means approval.
3. Use each guide's prerequisites, configuration/review steps, evidence and verification.
   Customer-system changes happen outside Atesta under the system owner's change process.
4. Obtain authoritative, appropriately licensed requirement text; add reviewed applicability
   and mappings. No ISO/AICPA criteria are reconstructed from memory or copied without rights.
5. Complete real collection, documentary review and catalog release gates before enabling scores.

No evidence-upload, assignment, exception approval, legal assessment or remediation execution
workflow is implemented by these templates. The dashboard exposes preparation content and
missing work; those actions remain separate product tasks.
