**You can now add a Google Workspace connection and set it up from Conexiones.** (#425) The screen walks you through the setup:
- **Authorize Atesta:** it shows the two client IDs and their exact read-only scopes to authorize in Google, each with a copy button.
- **Name the super admin:** it asks for the super admin Atesta reads as and recommends one who already exists. The primary domain comes from their email.
- **Verify:** it explains that Google can take up to 24 hours to apply the access.
