**A suspended organization, or one whose 14-day trial ended without a plan, is held to what it may still do, through one check.** (#463) While suspended, every route answers 403 except the owners' export, closure and billing details (GDPR art. 20), API keys and the remote MCP endpoint included, and the worker runs no scan and sends no notice. After the trial, every write answers 403 `billing.trial_ended` until Atesta sets a plan; reads and export stay. Owners read their billing details, and operator actions show in Activity as the Atesta team.
