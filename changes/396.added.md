**Proposed design for the operator portal, where the Atesta team manages customer organizations from their metadata only.** (#396) ADR-017, ADR-012 amendment 8 and the operator-portal spec describe it. Staff will set plans and billing details, suspend and reactivate organizations, and handle export, closure and erasure. They work from a separate application with its own sign-in and database role, and never see customer content. Every change appears in the customer's own activity. Nothing is built yet.
