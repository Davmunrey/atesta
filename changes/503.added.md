**Runbooks for the operator portal, and a command to manage its staff.** (#503) New runbooks cover staff accounts (the first admin, adding colleagues, sign-in with an authenticator app or a passkey, lost devices and leavers), plans and trials, and the deploy checks for the portal. The offboarding runbook now goes through the portal's export request, closure and two-person erasure. A staff command adds, disables and re-enables portal staff, and records each change in the operator trail, where the portal's operations log shows it.
