**The database now has a separate operator role that reads organization metadata and never customer content.** (#445) `atesta_operator` gets column-level grants on a closed list (names, plans, billing details, member and connection counts, scan and API-key usage, audit timestamps) and no privilege at all on assessments, evidence, findings, incidents, policies or any other content table. Organizations gain a 14-day trial end, a suspension state and a derived closure origin, and billing details get their own table. The customer role can no longer change the suspension or trial columns.
